# Prometheus Phase 4 Chemistry Cost-to-Accuracy Campaign: Sanitized Public Evidence Release (`32` Molecular Configurations, `172` Live QPU Jobs, `4,812` PUBs, `3,756,032` Physical Shots)

## Overview: Problem, Measured Achievement, and Scope

- **Problem & Achievement:** Across **32 molecular configurations** and **172 live benchmark jobs** (`4,812` circuit submissions, `3,756,032` physical shots) on IBM and IQM hardware, Prometheus worked alongside Qiskit SABRE and Quantinuum TKET to reduce **charged hardware resources per confirmed accurate calculation (`<= 25.0 mHa`) by 51.8% to 60.0% vs. the best baseline** - both across **24 untouched holdout configurations (`Phase 4A`)** and during **live iterative optimization started from Hartree-Fock (`Phase 4B`)**.
- **Why It Matters:** Research teams run roughly twice as many confirmed chemistry calculations within the same hardware allowance, while hardware providers and software partners extract more useful output per processor window alongside established Qiskit SABRE and Quantinuum TKET workflows (`49/49` early measurement stops confirmed).
- **Scope & Verification:** Savings measure provider-charged QPU seconds and hardware credits rather than software or classical compute costs; single-provider Prometheus outperformed dual-provider splits (`42/72` in `Phase 4A`, `3/6` strict in `Phase 4B`). Verify every pre-execution lock, billing total, and trajectory step offline via [`verify_phase4_public_release.py`](../scripts/verify_phase4_public_release.py).
